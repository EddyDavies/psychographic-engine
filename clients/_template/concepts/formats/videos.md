# Video Ad Formats

### Subtitle B-Roll
- Stock or branded footage with text overlay telling the story
- Best for: Educational, Problem-Solution
- When to use: No UGC creators available, brand wants polished look

### UGC Talking Head
- Creator speaking directly to camera
- Best for: Social Proof, Problem-Solution, Before/After
- When to use: Most versatile format, works across awareness levels

### Voiceover
- Product footage or lifestyle shots with voiceover narration
- Best for: Mechanism, Authority, Educational
- When to use: When the product needs demonstration, premium feel

### POV / Label Read
- First-person perspective, often unboxing or using product
- Best for: Social Proof, Identity
- When to use: ASMR-style, tactile products, curiosity hooks

### Hook/Hold Structure

Every video ad follows this structure:

- **Hook (0-3s):** Pattern interrupt. Must stop the scroll. Visual or verbal hook (or both).
- **Hold (3-15s):** Build curiosity or tension. Why should they keep watching?
- **Payload (15-30s):** Deliver the core message. Framework + angle execution.
- **CTA (last 3-5s):** Clear call to action. Tell them exactly what to do next.

Rule: If the hook doesn't work, nothing else matters. Test hooks independently.
