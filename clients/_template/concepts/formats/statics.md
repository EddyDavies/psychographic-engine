# Static Ad Formats

### Post-It Note
- Handwritten-style text on a coloured background
- Best for: Problem-Solution, Educational
- When to use: Pattern interrupt, casual feel, "insider tip" positioning

### Meme
- Cultural reference with brand twist
- Best for: Us vs Them, Identity
- When to use: Younger demographics, high social sharing potential

### UGC-Style Photo
- Looks like a customer photo, not a brand ad
- Best for: Social Proof, Before/After
- When to use: When polished ads are fatiguing, authenticity needed

### Editorial/Magazine
- High-production layout, premium feel
- Best for: Authority, Mechanism
- When to use: Premium positioning, higher price points

### Product Grid
- Multiple products arranged in a clean layout
- Best for: Social Proof, Scarcity
- When to use: Bundle offers, collection launches, comparison

### Testimonial Card
- Customer quote with photo/name
- Best for: Social Proof, Authority
- When to use: Retargeting, Most Aware audiences
