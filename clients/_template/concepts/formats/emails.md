# Email Formats

### Plain Text
- Looks like a personal email, no design elements
- Best for: Problem-Solution, Identity, Educational
- When to use: High deliverability, personal feel, founder/CEO voice

### Visual Hero
- Large hero image + short copy
- Best for: Social Proof, Scarcity, Before/After
- When to use: Product launches, seasonal campaigns, visual products

### Listicle
- Numbered list format ("5 reasons...", "3 mistakes...")
- Best for: Educational, Mechanism
- When to use: Nurture sequences, value-first content

### Storytelling
- Narrative arc with emotional payoff
- Best for: Hero/Villain, Identity, Before/After
- When to use: Brand building, longer sequences, high-ticket products
