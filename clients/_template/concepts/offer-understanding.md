# Offer Understanding

Know exactly what the product or offer is and does, so you can identify desires, build avatars, and write ads that match what it actually delivers. Evolve calls this the Product Overview Document. Fill it in `../products/{product}.md` (from `../products/_template.md`) before building avatars or angles for a new product, and in `../offers/{offer}.md` for a bundle.

## The seven steps

1. **Basics.** Name and what it costs the customer (money, time, effort, switching from what they use now).
2. **Offer in action.** HOW it works: the process and the outcome. Then rewrite it at a 6th grade reading level. Pattern: "X does Y, which causes Z." Fernwood: "Drop one pod in the bottle and add tap water, which makes a full bottle of spray without buying a new one."
3. **Features.** The main features, as many as needed. What it IS, not what it does for the customer.
4. **Assumed benefits.** For each feature: what does it DO for the customer? Several features may share a benefit.
5. **Assumed desires.** For each benefit: why does someone want or need that? Several benefits may lead to one desire. Map each to a Mass Instinct.
6. **New mechanisms.** What makes the product unique? Is there anything genuinely new that separates it from what the market has seen? See the market sophistication section of `marketing-frameworks.md`.
7. **Hidden mechanisms.** Four questions that surface what you are blind to:
   - What does the product do that you do not advertise?
   - What does it include that alternatives do not?
   - If it worked too well, what would people complain about?
   - If someone explained it to a friend, what would they say?

## Rules

- Benefits and desires are **assumed**. They are hypotheses until research or test results confirm them; mark them so, and check them against the scored desires in `../strategy/desires.md` before relying on one.
- The chain runs feature, benefit, desire. A feature with no desire at the end of it does not belong in the ad.
- The 6th grade version is the one the ads borrow from.
- An angle's Proof Points may only use claims this file supports.
