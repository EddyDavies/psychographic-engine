# Marketing Frameworks

Each framework paired with its ideal awareness level:

| Framework | Best For | Awareness Level |
|---|---|---|
| Problem-Solution | Revealing the problem, product as solution | Problem Aware |
| Hero/Villain | Position villain (ingredient/habit/competitor), product as hero | Solution Aware |
| Us vs Them | In-group identity creation | Product Aware |
| Before/After | Show transformation (Meta compliance note: avoid misleading before/after imagery) | Problem -> Solution Aware |
| Social Proof | Volume of happy customers, UGC | Product -> Most Aware |
| Authority | Expert endorsement, credentials | Solution Aware |
| Mechanism | Unique "how" (ingredient, process, tech) | Solution Aware (Stage 3+ markets) |
| Identity | "This is for people who ___" | Problem Aware |
| Scarcity | Limited availability | Most Aware |
| Educational | Teach value, product as natural next step | Unaware -> Problem Aware |

## Three Strategic Responses for Sophisticated Markets (Stage 3-5)

### 1. New Mechanism
Reveal the "secret thing" behind your solution. Tech, process, or ingredient angle. Works when the market has heard every claim but hasn't seen your HOW.

### 2. New Information
Educate to create problem awareness. Reframe the problem entirely. Works when the market thinks they understand the problem but doesn't.

### 3. New Identity
Lifestyle movement, tribal belonging. Redefine what buying says about the customer. Works when every logical argument has been exhausted.

## Market Sophistication Stages (Schwartz)

- **Stage 1: First to Market** -- Simple claims work. "This product does X."
- **Stage 2: Competition Emerges** -- Amplify promises. "This does X BETTER."
- **Stage 3: Market Saturation** -- Need new mechanisms. "This does X through [unique process]."
- **Stage 4: Mechanism Competition** -- Enhance/elaborate mechanisms. "Our [process] is better because..."
- **Stage 5: Complete Sophistication** -- Only emotional connection and identity works. "For the kind of person who..."

**Most eCommerce markets are Stage 3-5.** Simple claims and amplified promises are already exhausted. This is why avatar work alone isn't enough -- even with perfect avatars and clear desires, you fail if you can't break through the sophistication level.

### The Avatar-Market Sophistication Gap

Avatars tell you WHO to talk to and WHAT they want. Market sophistication tells you HOW to say it. Both are required:

- **Great avatar + wrong sophistication response** = message that understands the customer but sounds like every other brand. Gets ignored.
- **Right sophistication response + wrong avatar** = clever creative that speaks to no one in particular. Gets attention but doesn't convert.
- **Great avatar + right sophistication response** = message that feels personally relevant AND cuts through market noise. This is what wins.

Use the Three Strategic Responses (above) matched to your market's sophistication stage. At Stage 3+, default to New Mechanism. At Stage 5, lean on New Identity.
