# Format: Relatable Hook

> Bold situation-naming headline with benefit bullet list and large product hero on a clean background.
> Adapted from: 07-relatable-hook

## Variables

| Variable | Description | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| `reference_images` | Product photo (styled or cutout) | `products/images/starter-kit/` |
| `headline` | Situation-naming headline (under 30 chars) | "CUPBOARD FULL OF BOTTLES?" |
| `bullet_1` | First benefit with + prefix | "One glass bottle per room, refilled for years" |
| `bullet_2` | Second benefit | "One pod makes one 500ml bottle" |
| `bullet_3` | Third benefit | "Plant-based, no harsh chemical smell" |
| `bullet_4` | Fourth benefit | "Streak-free on glass and mirrors" |
| `bullet_5` | Fifth benefit (optional) | "60 pods a year for the price of 12 bottles" |
| `bullet_6` | Sixth benefit (optional) | "Over 4,000 five-star reviews" |
| `accent_color` | Colour for + bullet prefix | "#D4A853" |

## Prompt

### [FORMAT]

9:16 vertical portrait OR 1:1 square. Clean, bright composition on white or very light background.

### [COPY_OVERLAY]

Top area — large bold condensed sans-serif headline, black (#1A1A1A):
"{headline}"
This names the SITUATION, not the product features.

Below headline — 4-6 benefit bullets, each prefixed with a coloured ({accent_color}) "+" symbol:
+ {bullet_1}
+ {bullet_2}
+ {bullet_3}
+ {bullet_4}
Dark gray (#333333) text, clean sans-serif, scannable.

### [PRODUCTS]

Bottom half — large, aspirational product image from reference. Product is the hero — well-lit, showing texture and quality. On white/light background. Product should feel premium and desirable. Takes up at least 40% of the frame.

### [STYLE]

Clean, trustworthy direct-response ad. The headline creates instant relatability — the reader sees their situation named. The bullet list scans in 2 seconds. The product hero at the bottom is the payoff. Not cluttered, not moody — bright, clean, confident. Like a premium brand's Facebook ad that actually converts.

### [LEARNINGS]

- The headline MUST name a situation or pain, not a product feature. "CUPBOARD FULL OF BOTTLES?" works. "CONCENTRATE REFILL SYSTEM" doesn't.
- "+" prefix bullets render reliably — more reliable than custom icons or checkmarks
- 4-6 bullets is the sweet spot. Fewer than 4 feels thin; more than 6 gets cluttered
- White/light background is critical for this format — dark backgrounds change the entire feel
- Product hero at the bottom needs to be large — at least 40% of frame. Small product images kill the format.
- Styled product images work better than cutouts here — adds warmth
