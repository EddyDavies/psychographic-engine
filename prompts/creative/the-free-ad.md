# Format: The Free Ad

> "Get X free" headline with scattered benefit pills and multi-product arrangement on a warm lifestyle background.
> Adapted from: 09-the-free-ad

## Variables

| Variable | Description | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| `reference_images` | Multi-product arrangement (styled preferred) | `offers/images/starter-kit-bundle/` |
| `headline` | Free offer headline (under 30 chars) | "GET 3 MONTHS OF PODS FREE" |
| `headline_emoji` | Single excitement emoji | "🤩" |
| `benefit_1` | Desire-focused pill text (under 25 chars) | "A cupboard with room in it" |
| `benefit_2` | Desire-focused pill text | "Streak-free mirrors" |
| `benefit_3` | Desire-focused pill text | "No harsh chemical smell" |
| `benefit_4` | Desire-focused pill text | "Less plastic in the bin" |
| `benefit_5` | Desire-focused pill text | "Peace of mind with kids" |
| `benefit_6` | Desire-focused pill text | "Clean that actually works" |
| `accent_color` | Pill background colour | "#D4A853" |

## Prompt

### [FORMAT]

9:16 vertical portrait OR 1:1 square.

### [BACKGROUND]

Soft, defocused lifestyle scene — shallow depth of field, natural warm light, warm tones. Optional — can also be a clean solid light background. The background adds warmth without competing with products.

### [PRODUCTS]

Multi-product arrangement from reference image, centred in the frame. Main product prominent, secondary items supporting. Products feel generous, like a bundle worth having.

### [COPY_OVERLAY]

Top area — bold black or white headline with emoji:
{headline_emoji} "{headline}"
Large, bold, excitement-driven.

Scattered around the products — 6-8 rounded benefit pills with accent-coloured ({accent_color}) background and white text:
"{benefit_1}", "{benefit_2}", "{benefit_3}", "{benefit_4}", "{benefit_5}", "{benefit_6}"
Pills float around the product arrangement like a "feature explosion" — not in a neat list.

### [STYLE]

Exciting, generous, value-driven. The "free" headline creates instant interest. The scattered pills create visual energy. The product arrangement shows what you get. Not discount-bin — premium generosity. Like an exciting gift-with-purchase offer, not a clearance sale.

### [LEARNINGS]

- Benefit pills must name DESIRES not features — "A cupboard with room in it" not "Contains plant-based surfactants"
- "Scattered" pills render ~50% of the time — NanoBanana sometimes arranges them in a neat list instead. Both work.
- Keep pill text under 4 words each — longer text causes pills to overlap
- The emoji in the headline adds significant visual energy — always include it
- Multi-product reference images work best — single product looks thin for a "free" offer
- Styled reference images with warm backgrounds integrate better than cutouts for this format
