# This Is Why You Experience Pain Point

Educational hook that explains the root cause of a common frustration. Positions the brand as knowledgeable and the product as the informed solution.

## Structure

"This is why [you experience pain point]. [Brief explanation of the cause]. [Product as the fix]."

## Image Examples

- "This is why your mirrors are still streaky after you clean them."
- "This is why your eco spray doesn't actually clean."
- "This is why your bathroom smells like a swimming pool after you clean it."

## Ad Copy Examples

- "This is why your mirrors are still streaky after you clean them. Most glass sprays leave a film of residue that dries into smears. A residue-free formula evaporates clean, so there's nothing left to streak. No buffing, no newspaper, no second wipe."
- "This is why your eco spray doesn't actually clean. A lot of them are mostly water with very little cleaning agent in them. A concentrate pod puts the full dose in every bottle, so you get a proper clean without the harsh chemical smell."
