# The Secret to X

Positions the product as insider knowledge — a hidden answer to a common desire. Creates curiosity and implies exclusivity.

## Structure

"The secret to [desired outcome]? [Product/benefit framed as the simple answer]."

## Image Examples

- "The secret to streak-free mirrors."
- "The secret to looking put-together without trying."
- "The secret to a cupboard with one bottle in it."

## Ad Copy Examples

- "The secret to streak-free mirrors? It's not newspaper. It's not elbow grease. It's a glass cleaner that doesn't leave residue behind. That's it."
- "The secret to a tidy cleaning cupboard? Stop buying bottles. Every friend I've shown this to has said the same thing: 'Wait, it's just a pod and water?'"
