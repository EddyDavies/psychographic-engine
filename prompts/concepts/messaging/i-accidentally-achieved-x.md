# I Accidentally Achieved X

Frames the benefit as an unexpected side effect with a demographic anchor for relatability.

## Structure

"I accidentally [achieved X] at [demographic callout e.g. age]."

## Image Examples

- "I accidentally cleared out the cupboard under the sink at 43."
- "I accidentally reduced my stress levels by 50% at 23."
- "I accidentally got my husband cleaning the bathroom at 47."

## Ad Copy Examples

- "I accidentally cleared out the cupboard under the sink at 43. All I did was switch to refill pods. Eleven half-used plastic bottles went in the recycling and I haven't bought another one since."
- "I accidentally got my mum off bleach at 52. Left a Starter Kit at hers on a whim. She called me the next week to ask where to order more pods."
