# This Isn't X, It's X

Rejects the obvious category and reframes the product as something better or more aspirational.

## Structure

"This isn't [obvious thing 1–3 words]. It's [aspirational/emotional reframe 1–3 words]."

## Image Examples

- "This isn't a spray bottle. It's peace of mind."
- "This isn't a refill. It's a tidy cupboard."
- "This isn't a cleaner. It's one less worry."

## Ad Copy Examples

- "This isn't a cleaning spray. It's letting the kids and the dog back in the kitchen straight after you wipe the floor."
- "This isn't a refill subscription. It's never running out of glass cleaner the morning people are coming over."
