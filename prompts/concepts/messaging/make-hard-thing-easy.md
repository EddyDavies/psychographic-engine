# Make [Doing Something Hard] Easy

Positions the product as the shortcut that removes the difficulty from a task the audience already wants to do but finds hard.

## Structure

"Make [specific hard thing] easy."

## Image Examples

- "Make eating only 1200 calories easy."
- "Make cleaning a five-bedroom house without harsh chemicals easy."
- "Make sticking to a skincare routine with three kids easy."

## Ad Copy Examples

- "Make cleaning the whole house without harsh chemicals easy, even when life gets messy. One bottle per room, drop in a pod, add water, done."
- "Make sticking to 1200 calories easy. Meals that actually fill you up so you stop white-knuckling it by 3pm."
