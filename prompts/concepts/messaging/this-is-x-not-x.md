# This Is X, Not X

Leads with the elevated reframe, then distances it from the inferior alternative.

## Structure

"This is [elevated reframe]. Not [what they're used to]."

## Image Examples

- "This is a clean kitchen. Not a chemical smell."
- "This is one glass bottle. Not a cupboard of plastic."
- "This is plant-based. Not harsh chemicals."

## Ad Copy Examples

- "This is a plant-based clean that actually shifts grease. Not the harsh stuff you grab off a supermarket shelf and then air the room out after."
- "This is a bottle you'll keep for years. Not another plastic one that ends up half-used at the back of the cupboard."
