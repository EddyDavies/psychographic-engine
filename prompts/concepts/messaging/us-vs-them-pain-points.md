# Us vs Them — They Have Pain Points

Lists the pain points of the alternatives. No comparison — just stacking up what's wrong with what they're currently using.

## Structure

"[Alternative]: [pain point]. [Alternative]: [pain point]."

## Image Examples

- "Supermarket sprays: another plastic bottle."
- "Bleach: that smell."
- "Eco brands: streaks."

## Ad Copy Examples

- "Supermarket sprays come in a new plastic bottle every time. Then it's half-used at the back of the cupboard while you buy another one. And another one. And another one."
- "Bleach sprays leave the bathroom smelling like a swimming pool. Antibacterial wipes go straight in the bin. Most eco sprays leave your mirrors streakier than before you started."
