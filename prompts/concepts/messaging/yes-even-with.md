# Yes, Even With [Obstacle]

Acknowledges a real-world obstacle the audience assumes will stop the product from working — then dismisses it. Builds credibility by showing you understand their actual life.

## Structure

"[Desirable outcome]. Even with [real obstacle]."

## Image Examples

- "Your bathroom can stay clean. Even with teenage boys."
- "You can stick to a budget. Even with a wedding to plan."
- "Your mirrors can stay streak-free. Even with three kids."

## Ad Copy Examples

- "Your kitchen can be properly clean. Even with two dogs, a teenager, and last night's curry. One bottle for the counters and you stop reaching for the bleach."
- "You can clean the floors without worrying. Even with a puppy who licks everything you've just wiped."
