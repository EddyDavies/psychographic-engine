# Us vs Them — We Have Unique Features

Lists your differentiators. Image is just the features stacked. Ad copy expands on why each one matters.

## Structure

"[Unique feature]. [Unique feature]. [Unique feature]. [Unique feature]."

## Image Examples

- "Plant-based formula."
- "One pod replaces one plastic bottle."
- "Streak-free glass."
- "No harsh chemical smell."

## Ad Copy Examples

- "One pod replaces one plastic bottle. Drop it in the glass bottle you already own, add water, and that's one less bottle in the recycling. Every single time."
- "Plant-based formula. Which means no harsh chemical smell, no airing the room out, no stinging eyes. Just a proper clean."
