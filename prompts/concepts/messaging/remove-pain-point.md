# Remove Pain Point

Directly names a specific, recognisable pain and promises to get rid of it.

## Structure

"Get rid of [specific pain point]."

## Image Examples

- "Get rid of streaky mirrors."
- "Get rid of that harsh chemical smell."
- "Get rid of the cupboard full of half-used bottles."

## Ad Copy Examples

- "Get rid of that harsh chemical smell that hangs around the bathroom for an hour after you clean it. One pod, one bottle, and a clean that doesn't make your eyes water."
- "Get rid of that moment of panic when the toddler opens the cupboard under the sink and you're not sure what's in half those bottles."
