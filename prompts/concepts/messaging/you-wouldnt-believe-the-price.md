# You Wouldn't Believe the Price

Teases the price without revealing it — creates a curiosity gap that drives click-through.

## Structure

"[Value statement]. [Price tease without revealing it]."

## Image Examples

- "4 bottles. You won't believe the price."
- "Plant-based. Refillable. Streak-free. Check the price."
- "60 pods a year. Wait till you see what it costs."

## Ad Copy Examples

- "Four glass bottles and a stack of plant-based pods in one Starter Kit. I'm not going to tell you the price because you genuinely won't believe me. Just check."
- "Plant-based. Streak-free. 60 pods a year for the price of 12 bottles. You'd expect to pay a fortune. You won't. Go look."
