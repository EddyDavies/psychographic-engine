# Format: Accidental Ideal Outcome

> Casual "I accidentally achieved [outcome]" headline on dark background with product photo and bullet points.
> Adapted from: 06-accidental-ideal-outcome

## Variables

| Variable | Description | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| `reference_images` | Clean product shot (cutout preferred) | `products/images/spray-bottle/cutout.jpg` |
| `headline_line_1` | First line of casual headline | "I Accidentally Emptied" |
| `headline_line_2` | Second line with specific detail | "The Cupboard Under My Sink" |
| `bullet_1` | First benefit (with visual verb) | "Replaced 11 half-used plastic bottles" |
| `bullet_2` | Second benefit | "One pod makes one 500ml bottle" |
| `bullet_3` | Third benefit | "Plant-based, no harsh chemical smell" |
| `disclaimer_text` | Small disclaimer (optional) | "Individual results may vary." |

## Prompt

### [FORMAT]

9:16 vertical portrait OR 1:1 square. Dark charcoal background (#333333 to #444444).

### [BACKGROUND]

Solid dark charcoal background — warm, not cold. Subtle texture or noise is fine but no patterns, no gradients, no environment.

### [PRODUCTS]

Product from reference image displayed on the left side of the frame with a thin white border frame around it. Clean, well-lit product shot standing out against the dark background. Product takes up roughly 40% of the frame width.

### [COPY_OVERLAY]

Top area — headline in white, casual/handwritten-feeling font, large:
"{headline_line_1}"
"{headline_line_2}"
The "handwritten" quality makes it feel personal, like a real person's story.

Right of product — three bullet points in white bold sans-serif:
• {bullet_1}
• {bullet_2}
• {bullet_3}

Bottom — small light gray (#999999) disclaimer:
"{disclaimer_text}"

### [STYLE]

Feels like a real person sharing an accidental discovery on social media. The dark background and casual font create intimacy. The bullet points provide scannable proof. Not salesy, not clinical — personal and surprising. The "accidentally" framing makes the outcome feel effortless.

### [LEARNINGS]

- Use SPECIFIC, non-round numbers for credibility — "11 bottles" not "loads of bottles", "4,000+ reviews" not "thousands of reviews"
- The handwritten/casual font renders inconsistently — sometimes NanoBanana uses a standard font. The instruction helps but isn't guaranteed.
- White border frame around product renders reliably and helps the product pop on dark backgrounds
- Keep bullets to 3 max — more than 3 clutters the layout and NanoBanana starts overlapping text
- The "accidentally" framing MUST be in the headline — it's the curiosity mechanism. Without it, this is just another benefit ad.
- Cutout product images work best for this format — in-scene photos compete with the dark background
