# UGC Whiteboard Kitchen

Fake-UGC static — a person holding a whiteboard with a message, products visible in the foreground. Designed to look like an unpolished photo shared in a family group chat.

## When to Use

Pattern interrupt for cold audiences. Works best with short, punchy benefit claims or social proof stats on the whiteboard. The casual, low-fi aesthetic stops the scroll by looking like organic content, not an ad.

## Inputs

| Placeholder | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `[WHITEBOARD TEXT]` | Short message written on the board | "Haven't bought a plastic spray bottle in a year" |
| Reference image | Product photo to reproduce in the scene | fernwood-starter-kit.png |

## Prompt Template

```
[CAMERA]: An unedited, low-resolution vertical photo from a 2018 smartphone. Visible digital noise/grain in the shadows. Slight wide-angle lens distortion. The image is a bit "flat" with no professional color grading.
[SUBJECT]: A happy British man in his late 40s, posing to smile at the camera, as if his friend is taking the photo. He is wearing a polo. His posture is slightly slumped and natural. His face is fully visible with no shadows.
[ACTION/POSING]: He is holding a whiteboard with both hands, gripping the sides clumsily. On the board, "[WHITEBOARD TEXT]" is written in messy, uneven black permanent marker. The board is fully visible with nothing in front of it, and is partially cutting off his shoulder.
[THE PRODUCTS]: Show all the products in the reference image. They are placed in front so they are all visible but not perfectly centered.
[THE ENVIRONMENT]: A cute quaint lived-in British kitchen during the daytime. The lighting is from sunlight from a window making the scene bright overall. The background is "busy" with authentic details: a white toaster with a stray cord, a drying rack with a few mismatched mugs, a white radiator, and a light switch on the wall.
[VIBE]: A "cute" photo sent in a family group chat. No symmetry, no "AI glow," no dramatic lighting. Just a raw, candid home moment.
```
