# Awareness & Sophistication Levels: Fernwood

> Fictional worked example.

## Awareness Levels (Eugene Schwartz)

| Level | Definition | What the ad must do | Sub-Avatars |
|-------|-----------|--------------------|--------------------|
| **Unaware** | Doesn't know they have a problem | Create the problem | |
| **Problem Aware** | Knows the problem, not the solutions | Educate on the solution category | `sub-flat-share-no-storage`, `sub-toddler-opening-cupboards`, `sub-weaning-high-chair` |
| **Solution Aware** | Knows solutions exist, doesn't know your product | Differentiate: "why THIS one" | `sub-let-down-eco-switcher`, `sub-subscription-sceptic` |
| **Product Aware** | Knows your product, hasn't bought yet | Overcome objections, provide offer | Retargeting pools from any sub-avatar |
| **Most Aware** | Knows your product well, needs a push | Offer, urgency, new product | Starter Kit buyers, for `refill-subscription` |

## Market Sophistication

Refill concentrates and eco sprays are a crowded category in the UK. The market has heard "plastic-free", "plant-based", "eco", "refill and save" and "kind to the planet" many times over, and a large share of buyers has tried at least one green cleaner and gone back.

- **Stage:** 4. Evidence (illustrative): many brands sell refill tablets, concentrates or eco sprays; all of them claim the same benefits (less plastic, plant-based, cheaper per bottle); several already explain a mechanism ("just add water"). Simple claims and amplified promises are exhausted, and the leading complaint in review mining is that eco sprays do not clean as well.
- **Chosen response:** New mechanism, made demonstrable. Fernwood does not claim to be greener; it claims the full-dose dry pod cleans like the bottle you gave up, and proves it on glass. Supporting response: new information ("ready-mixed spray is mostly water", "streaks are leftover residue"). New identity (the thoughtful household) is kept for visuals, not for the promise.

> Method: `engine/method/marketing-frameworks.md`. Angles must rest on the chosen response, not on bigger claims.
