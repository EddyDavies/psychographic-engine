# Mass Instincts: Fernwood

> Fictional worked example.

The 6 permanent forces that drive all purchasing decisions.

| Instinct | Definition | Fernwood Expression |
|----------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Health** | Desire for physical wellbeing, safety, avoiding harm | Knowing what is in the bottles a child can reach or a baby eats near. Expressed through facts (short ingredient list, no chlorine bleach, unscented option, lidded tin), never through safety claims. Anchor of `core-careful-parent` |
| **Status** | Desire to be respected, admired, envied | Self-respect more than admiration: being the household that quietly stopped buying plastic; glass bottles on display; a mirror that looks properly clean when people come round. Anchor of `core-low-waste-household` |
| **Sex** | Desire for attractiveness, intimacy, sensory pleasure | Weak fit. Only the sensory side: the Lemon Peel scent, the look of the sage glass bottles. Not used as a lead |
| **Comfort** | Desire for ease, relaxation, removal of pain | A proper clean without the smell, the shop trip or the clutter; drop a pod in, add water, done |
| **Control** | Desire for certainty, mastery, ending the search | Ending the search for a green cleaner that works; never running out; never being locked into a plan; a tidy cupboard |
| **Belonging** | Desire for connection, community, being part of something | Weak fit. Shared flats where the whole flat uses the same four bottles. Not used as a lead |
