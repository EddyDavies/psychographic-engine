# Forces of Change: Fernwood

> Fictional worked example. Forces described generally; no real studies or brands are cited.

## Mass Education

| ID | Force | Activates Instinct | Fit |
|----|-------|--------------------|-----|
| force-recycling-doubt | Growing public awareness that putting plastic in the recycling does not mean it gets recycled, so "just recycle it" no longer settles the guilt | Status | Strong |
| force-label-reading | Parents increasingly read the back of household products and look up ingredient names they do not recognise | Health | Strong |
| force-green-scepticism | Shoppers have learned that "eco" on a label can mean very little, and distrust vague green claims | Control | Strong (Fernwood's specific claims and proof-first voice ride it; vague competitors are hurt by it) |
| force-bleach-isnt-for-limescale | Cleaning creators teaching that acids, not bleach, shift limescale | Comfort | Moderate |

## Style Trends

| ID | Force | Activates Instinct | Fit |
|----|-------|--------------------|-----|
| force-decanted-cupboard | Matching decanted bottles and tidy "cleaning cupboard" content on social video | Status | Strong |
| force-glass-over-plastic | Glass bottles and jars on display in kitchens as a sign of a considered home | Status | Moderate |
| force-cleaning-as-content | Satisfying cleaning videos (streak-free glass, shiny taps) as entertainment | Comfort | Strong (gives the glass demo a native format) |

## Mass Technological Problems

| ID | Force | Applied to Category | Emotion |
|----|-------|--------------------| --------|
| force-complexity-one-bottle-per-surface | Complexity | A separate ready-mixed bottle for every surface, so the cupboard fills with half-used plastic | Overwhelm (Fear) |
| force-maintenance-running-out | Maintenance | Sprays run out at the worst moment and need a trip to the shop | Frustration (Anger) |
| force-fragility-trigger-heads | Fragility | Cheap trigger heads break before the bottle is empty, so the whole thing gets binned | Annoyance (Anger) |
| force-subscription-creep | Overwhelm | Too many small subscriptions that are hard to track and cancel | Distrust (Fear) |
