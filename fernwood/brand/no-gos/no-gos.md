# Brand No-Gos

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

### Claims to Never Make
- "Kills 99.9% of germs", "antibacterial", "disinfects" or any germ claim. The pods are cleaners, not tested or registered as disinfectants
- "Non-toxic", "chemical-free" or "safe for babies, children or pets". Everything is a chemical, and safety claims need evidence we do not have. Say "plant-based formula" and "no chlorine bleach or ammonia" instead
- "Safe if swallowed" or anything that makes a pod sound edible
- "Plastic-free" for the whole range. The bottle sleeves are silicone. Say "no new plastic bottle", which is true
- Vague green claims ("eco-friendly", "sustainable", "good for the planet") with no specific fact behind them. Every environmental claim names the specific thing (one pod replaces one plastic bottle) so it holds up against UK green-claims guidance
- "Works on everything". Name the surfaces each pod is for
- Superlatives with no proof ("the best cleaner in the UK")
- Exact savings against a named product. Compare against "a typical £4 supermarket spray" only

### Visual No-Gos
- Children holding, touching or near pods, the tin or a filled bottle
- Pods near food, cups or anyone's mouth
- Before/after images implying impossible results (a black mould wall wiped spotless in one pass)
- Other brands' bottles, logos or label colours, even blurred
- Dead turtles, plastic in the sea, landfill drone shots or any planet-guilt imagery
- Cold, clinical, stock-looking bathrooms

### Tone No-Gos
- Guilt or fear about the planet ("your bottles are choking the ocean")
- Fear about children ("is your cleaner poisoning your kids?"). Parents get reassurance and facts, never scares
- Talking down to people who went back to their old spray. They were let down; it was not their fault
- Overly salesy language ("crazy deal", "act now")
- Categorising ourselves as "an eco spray"

### Competitor References
- **Policy:** Never mention by name. Can reference the category generally ("most eco sprays", "a typical supermarket spray") and compare features without naming anyone.

### Platform Compliance
- **Meta Ad Policy:** No misleading claims, no personal attributes ("Are you a messy parent?"), no before/after implying impossible results, no sensationalist content
- **Category-Specific:** Household cleaning products carry hazard and storage guidance. Ads showing the tin must not contradict "keep out of reach of children". No medical or allergy claims ("good for asthma") even when customers say it in reviews
