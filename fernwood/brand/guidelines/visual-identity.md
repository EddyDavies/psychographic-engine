# Visual Identity Guidelines

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

### Color Palette
| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | #8A9A7B | Sage. Bottle sleeves, backgrounds, the dominant colour of every product shot |
| Secondary | #D4A853 | Warm gold. Annotation lines, bullet markers, price callouts, CTAs |
| Text | #1F2A24 | Deep fern. Headlines and body copy |
| Background | #F3EEE4 | Oat. Page and card backgrounds, studio sweeps |
| Kraft | #C8A97E | Kit box sleeve and paper refill pouch; use as a warm surface in flat lays |

### Typography
| Role | Font | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Heading | Fraunces (soft serif) | Headlines, hero text, ad headlines; sentence case or short all-caps lines |
| Body | Inter | Body copy, descriptions, captions, call outs |
| Accent | Fraunces Italic | One or two word emphasis ("clean, refill, repeat"); never full sentences |

### Photography Style
- **Lighting:** Natural, soft morning window light
- **Composition:** Lifestyle in real homes (oak worktops, tiled splashbacks, the cupboard under the sink); flat lays on kraft or linen for kit shots
- **Mood:** Calm, lived-in, tidy but not staged
- **Color Treatment:** Warm tones, muted greens, no cold blue bathroom light
- **People:** Hands-only by default (dropping a pod in, wiping a mirror). Adults only when a face is needed. Never children holding a pod, a bottle or the tin

### Logo Usage
- **Minimum Size:** 24px tall on a 1080px wide ad
- **Clear Space:** The height of the "F" on every side
- **Placement on Ads:** Bottom-right, or on the bottle sleeve in product shots only
- **Color Variants:** Deep fern on light backgrounds, oat on sage or dark backgrounds

### Ad-Specific Rules
- **Text Overlay Opacity:** 60% deep fern overlay when text sits on a busy photo
- **Text-to-Image Ratio:** Under 20% text on image where possible
- **CTA Button Styling:** Gold fill, deep fern text, rounded 8px corners, sentence case ("See the kit")
- **Border/Frame:** No
- **Product reference images:** `products/images/` and `offers/images/` (the kit photo is `fernwood-starter-kit.png`); always reproduce the real kit, never describe it in text for the image model
