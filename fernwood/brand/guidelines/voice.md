# Brand Voice Guidelines

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

### Brand Voice Summary
> Fernwood sounds like the friend who has already tried every cleaning product and will tell you straight which one works. Plain, warm, a bit dry. It talks about streaks, smells and cupboard space, never about saving the planet, because the customer already feels guilty enough and has been let down by green promises before.

### Tone Attributes
| Attribute | Like This | Not Like This |
|---|---|---|
| Plain-spoken | "One pod. Tap water. Streak-free glass." | "Harness the power of plant-based innovation." |
| Proof first | "Spray the mirror. Wipe once. Look." | "The most effective eco cleaner on the market." |
| Dry, not jokey | "Your cleaning cupboard doesn't need eleven bottles. It needs four." | "Say bye-bye to boring bottles, besties!" |
| Warm, no guilt | "Fewer bottles in the recycling. Same clean." | "Every bottle you buy ends up in the ocean." |
| Practical | "Works in any 500ml spray bottle you already own." | "Join the refill revolution." |
| Honest about limits | "It's a cleaner, not a disinfectant." | "Kills everything, safe for everyone." |

### Language Register
- **Formality:** Conversational
- **Jargon Policy:** Avoid entirely. Say "cleaning agent", not "surfactant blend"; say "the fizzy tablet", not "effervescent concentrate"
- **Reading Level:** 6th grade or below; short sentences, one idea each
- **Contractions:** Yes
- **First Person:** "We" for the brand; customer stories in "I"
- **Spelling:** British English (colour, mould, flatmate)

### Example Phrases
> - "Drop a pod in. Add tap water. That's a full bottle."
> - "It's not another eco spray. It's the pod that makes the bottle you already own clean like the one you gave up."
> - "Most of a spray bottle is water. So we stopped posting you water."
> - "If it doesn't clean as well as what you used before, we'll refund the kit. Keep the bottles."
> - "Four bottles. One tin. The rest of the cupboard is yours again."

### Platform-Specific Adjustments
- **Meta Ads:** Shorter, punchier. Lead with the scene or the proof (the mirror, the cupboard), not the brand. Two or three sentences of primary text at most.
- **Email:** Can be longer and more personal. Founder story allowed. Still no planet guilt.
- **Landing Page:** Proof above the fold (glass demo, guarantee), then how it works in three steps, then price per bottle.
