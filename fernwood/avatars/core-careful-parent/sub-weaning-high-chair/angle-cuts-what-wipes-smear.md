# Angle: Cuts What Baby Wipes Smear

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** The all-purpose spray lifts the greasy film that baby wipes just push around the tray, so they stop going through a pack of wipes a day and still not getting it clean.

**Emotional Lever:** The small daily defeat of the greasy tray. Profile: Behaviour "reaching for baby wipes or plain water because they are unsure about spray" and the Full Description ("baby wipes that never quite cut the grease"). It gives them permission to use a spray by solving the problem the wipes leave.

## Hooks

> Hooks communicate the angle. Keep them DIRECT (not indirect).
> Each hook should be usable as a headline on a static ad or opening line of a video.

1. **"Baby wipes don't clean the tray."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm from the profile behaviour; quirky hook framework

2. **"One wipe. No greasy film."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm from the product (`products/all-purpose-spray.md`, cuts everyday kitchen grease)

3. **"Still smeary after six wipes?"**
   inspired_by: brainstorm; validates the pain

## Proof Points

> Only claims the product or offer file supports, each with where it comes from. Never fabricate volumes, names, or stories (see `engine/method/copywriting.md`).

- Cuts everyday kitchen grease in one or two wipes (source: `products/all-purpose-spray.md`)
- Unscented option, full ingredient list on the tin (source: `products/all-purpose-spray.md`)
- One pod makes one 500ml bottle (source: `products/all-purpose-spray.md`)
- Not claimed: anything negative about a named wipe brand (see `brand/no-gos/no-gos.md`); compare to "baby wipes" as a category only

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "Baby wipes are easier": a bottle that lives by the high chair is as quick, and it actually lifts the grease
- "Whatever I spray on that tray ends up in her mouth": one line on the Unscented pod and the printed list
