# Angle: Wipe All You Like at 80p a Bottle

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** On the refill subscription a full bottle costs 80p, so they can spray the tray, table and floor after every meal without rationing it or reaching for the wipes to save money.

**Emotional Lever:** The quiet rationing of something used constantly. Profile: Behaviour "reaching for baby wipes or plain water" and the volume of wiping ("three or more times a day"). At this volume price per bottle becomes a daily feeling, not a one-off decision.

## Hooks

> Hooks communicate the angle. Keep them DIRECT (not indirect).
> Each hook should be usable as a headline on a static ad or opening line of a video.

3. **"Three meals a day. Three wipes. 80p."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm from the profile situation

> Only claims the product or offer file supports, each with where it comes from. Never fabricate volumes, names, or stories (see `engine/method/copywriting.md`).

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "Baby wipes are easier": and dearer per clean at this volume; the bottle by the high chair is just as quick
- "Unscented means it isn't really cleaning": one line on grease lifting in one wipe
