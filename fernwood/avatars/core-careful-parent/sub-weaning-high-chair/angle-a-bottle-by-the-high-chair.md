# Angle: A Bottle That Lives by the High Chair

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** The kit's four bottles mean one Unscented bottle can live permanently by the high chair, so it is in reach mid-mess instead of in the cupboard across the room.

**Emotional Lever:** The scramble after a meal with a baby in one arm. Profile: Objection "Baby wipes are easier" (convenience at the high chair, mid-mess) and the unexpected use from review mining ("keep a bottle by the high chair so it's always there"). The win is a small, practical habit customers already invented.
