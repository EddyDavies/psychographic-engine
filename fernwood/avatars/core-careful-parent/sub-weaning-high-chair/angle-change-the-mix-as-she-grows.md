# Angle: Change the Mix as She Grows

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** The refill subscription lets them set the pod mix (all Unscented all-purpose during weaning, more bathroom pods later) and skip a delivery when the tin is full, so the plan fits this stage of the baby and the next one.

**Emotional Lever:** Overwhelm at a stage that changes every few weeks. Profile (core): Secondary emotion "overwhelmed: every product seems to need a judgement call". A plan they can adjust in their account is one less thing to rethink each month.

## Hooks

> Hooks communicate the angle. Keep them DIRECT (not indirect).
> Each hook should be usable as a headline on a static ad or opening line of a video.

1. **"Weaning now. Bath time next."**

   inspired_by: review mining, overwhelm; validates the pain

## Proof Points

> Only claims the product or offer file supports, each with where it comes from. Never fabricate volumes, names, or stories (see `engine/method/copywriting.md`).

- Any mix of all-purpose, glass and bathroom pods per delivery (source: `offers/refill-subscription.md`)
- Skip, pause, change the mix or cancel online any time (source: `offers/refill-subscription.md`)
- Dry pods keep for two years, so a full tin is never wasted (source: `products/all-purpose-spray.md`)

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "Whatever I spray on that tray ends up in her mouth": the default weaning mix is Unscented all-purpose
