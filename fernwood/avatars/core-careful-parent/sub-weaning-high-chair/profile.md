# Sub-Avatar: sub-weaning-high-chair

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

## Parent Core Avatar
[core-careful-parent](../profile.md)

## Niche Gap
- **Situation:** They have just started weaning a baby and wipe the high chair tray, the table and the floor underneath after every meal, three or more times a day.
- **Unique Objection:** "Whatever I spray on that tray ends up in her mouth."
- **Different Value Prop:** The value is the surface the baby eats from: an unscented spray with a short ingredient list they can read, at a price that means they never ration it.
- **Three Questions:** different ad: yes (the high chair tray, mealtimes, volume of wiping); unique objection: yes (residue where the baby eats); situation alone: yes (weaning, wiping after every meal)

> The niche gap test (see `../../README.md`). All three must be yes. A "no" means the gap is weak: name the sub-avatar it overlaps and merge rather than keep it.

## Layered Categories
> Minimum 2 Core 5 categories combined
1. **Desire:** Know what is on the surface the baby eats from
2. **Experience:** Weaning; wiping the tray and floor after every meal
3. **Behaviour:** Reaching for baby wipes or plain water because they are unsure about spray

## Full Description
> One sentence combining all categories:
"They want the high chair properly clean, but they wipe it three times a day and the baby eats straight off it, so they feel uneasy about what the spray leaves behind and often fall back on baby wipes that never quite cut the grease."

## Implied Beliefs
> Decomposed from the belief "anything with a strong smell is too strong for where she eats":
- **Experience:** "Because the kitchen spray smell hung over the tray right before lunch..."
- **Emotion:** "...they felt uneasy and a bit guilty..."
- **Behavior:** "...so they use wipes or water on the tray and save the spray for the worktop."

## Hooks That Resonate
1. "Wiped three times a day. Licked twice.": their own joke, the real situation
2. "Nothing on the tray you can't read.": short ingredient list, unscented
3. "80p a bottle. Wipe all you like.": the volume they actually clean at
