# Angle: Unscented for the Tray

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** The Starter Kit comes with Unscented all-purpose pods and a short ingredient list on the tin, so the high chair tray gets properly cleaned with nothing perfumed left where the baby eats.

**Emotional Lever:** Unease about what is left on the tray. Profile: unique objection "Whatever I spray on that tray ends up in her mouth" and the belief "anything with a strong smell is too strong for where she eats". Unscented answers the signal they actually use (smell), and the printed list answers the question behind it.

## Hooks

> Hooks communicate the angle. Keep them DIRECT (not indirect).
> Each hook should be usable as a headline on a static ad or opening line of a video.

1. **"Nothing on the tray you can't read."**
   inspired_by: review mining, "I just want to know what's actually in the thing I'm spraying where she eats"

2. **"Wiped three times a day. Licked twice."**
   inspired_by: review mining, "I wipe that tray three times a day and then she licks it"; validates the pain in their words

3. **"Clean tray. No smell. Short label."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm from the product (`products/all-purpose-spray.md`, Unscented version)

## Proof Points

> Only claims the product or offer file supports, each with where it comes from. Never fabricate volumes, names, or stories (see `engine/method/copywriting.md`).

- Unscented all-purpose pod, no added fragrance; choose it in the kit (source: `products/all-purpose-spray.md`, `offers/starter-kit.md`)
- Full ingredient list printed on the tin (source: `products/all-purpose-spray.md`)
- For worktops, tables and high chair trays; cuts everyday kitchen grease (source: `products/all-purpose-spray.md`)
- Not claimed: food-safe, baby-safe, no-rinse, non-toxic (see `brand/no-gos/no-gos.md`)

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "Whatever I spray on that tray ends up in her mouth": never promise it is safe to eat; show the list and let them decide, and keep the image to a clean, empty tray
- "Unscented means it isn't really cleaning": show the grease lifting in one wipe; smell was never the cleaning
