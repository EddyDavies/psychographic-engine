# Angle: Never Run Out Mid-Mealtime

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** Weaning gets through all-purpose spray faster than anything else in the house, and the refill subscription posts 15 pods through the letterbox every three months, so the bottle is never empty when dinner is on the floor.

**Emotional Lever:** The moment of reaching for the spray with a baby covered in food and finding it empty. Profile: Experience "wiping the tray and floor after every meal" and the volume of wiping in the Full Description. On this offer the pain is not what is in the bottle but whether there is anything in it.

## Proof Points

> Only claims the product or offer file supports, each with where it comes from. Never fabricate volumes, names, or stories (see `engine/method/copywriting.md`).

- 15 pods every 3 months, any mix, letterbox-sized pouch (source: `offers/refill-subscription.md`)
- Change the mix toward all-purpose pods in your account (source: `offers/refill-subscription.md`, Basics)
- Skip, pause or cancel online any time (source: `offers/refill-subscription.md`)
- Customers run out of all-purpose pods first (source: `products/all-purpose-spray.md`, Hidden Mechanisms)

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "Baby wipes are easier": they are only easier when the spray has run out; the subscription stops that happening
- "Whatever I spray on that tray ends up in her mouth": choose Unscented in the mix, list on every pouch
