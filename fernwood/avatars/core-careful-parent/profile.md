# Core Avatar: core-careful-parent

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand; quotes come from invented research.

## Anchor Category
**Emotion:** A low, constant unease about what is in the cleaning products now that a small child can reach the cupboards and eats off the surfaces.

## Mass Instinct
Health. The desire underneath is "I need to know what's in the bottles my child can reach." Fernwood answers it with facts the parent can check (a short printed ingredient list, no chlorine bleach, an unscented option, a lidded tin), never with safety claims (see `../../brand/no-gos/no-gos.md`).

## Core 5 Profile

### Desires
- **Primary:** Know exactly what is in the cleaning products around their child
- Fewer things in the house that need hiding or locking away
- A clean kitchen without a chemical smell where the baby eats and crawls
- Feel like they have one corner of parenting under control

### Experiences
- Child has recently started crawling, walking, or opening cupboards
- Has started weaning, so surfaces get wiped many times a day
- Has read the back of a cleaning bottle and not understood the ingredients
- Has moved bottles to the top of the fridge or bought cupboard locks
- Has been told by another parent or a forum to "just use vinegar" and found it did not cut grease

### Emotions
**Primary Emotions:**
- Fear (unease): triggered by the child reaching for the cupboard under the sink, or by not recognising words on a label

**Secondary Emotions:**
- Overwhelmed: because every product seems to need a judgement call and there is no time to research them all (Fear)
- Guilty: because they cannot tell whether what they use is fine and feel they should know (Sadness)
- Relieved: when something is simple enough to stop thinking about (Joy)

### Behaviors
- Reads labels and searches ingredient names late at night
- Moves anything that seems harsh out of reach; fits cupboard locks
- Wipes the high chair, table and floor several times a day
- Asks other parents what they use rather than trusting brand claims

### Demographics
> Broad strokes only. Often implied by the above.
- 27 to 40
- Parents of children under four
- UK, mostly in homes with a kitchen cupboard under the sink

## Awareness Level
**Problem Aware**

> - **Unaware:** Doesn't know they have a problem
> - **Problem Aware:** Knows the problem, not the solutions
> - **Solution Aware:** Knows solutions exist, doesn't know your product
> - **Product Aware:** Knows your product, hasn't bought yet
> - **Most Aware:** Knows your product well, needs a push to buy
