# Angle: The Bleach Can Leave the House

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** The kit's bathroom pod shifts limescale with citric acid, so the bottle of bleach they worry about most no longer has to live under the sink.

**Emotional Lever:** One specific bottle carries most of the worry. Profile: Behaviour "moves anything that seems harsh out of reach" and the belief "the fewer bottles in reach, the better". Removing the one product they are most uneasy about is a concrete, checkable change, not a vague promise.

## Hooks

> Hooks communicate the angle. Keep them DIRECT (not indirect).
> Each hook should be usable as a headline on a static ad or opening line of a video.

1. **"Shiny taps. No bleach."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm from the product (`products/bathroom-spray.md`)

2. **"Bleach doesn't shift limescale. Acid does."**
   inspired_by: new information (`products/bathroom-spray.md`, Assumed New Information)

3. **"The bottle you hide first."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm; review mining, "everything harsh went on top of the fridge"

## Proof Points

> Only claims the product or offer file supports, each with where it comes from. Never fabricate volumes, names, or stories (see `engine/method/copywriting.md`).

- Bathroom pod uses citric acid for limescale; no chlorine bleach (source: `products/bathroom-spray.md`)
- Bleach whitens and disinfects; an acid removes limescale (source: `products/bathroom-spray.md`, Assumed New Information)
- Three bathroom pods in the kit (source: `offers/starter-kit.md`)
- Not claimed: that the pod disinfects or replaces bleach for germ killing (see `brand/no-gos/no-gos.md`)

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "Plant-based doesn't mean harmless": do not claim harmless; claim the specific swap (citric acid for the limescale job)
- "Glass bottles in a house with a toddler?": silicone sleeves on every bottle
