# Angle: Know Every Ingredient

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** Every pod's full ingredient list is short and printed on the tin, and they fill the bottles themselves, so they know exactly what is in every bottle in the house.

**Emotional Lever:** The late-night label reading. Profile (core): "I read the back of every bottle now and I don't understand half the words." Profile (sub): Objection "Plant-based doesn't mean harmless". The angle does not ask them to trust "plant-based"; it gives them the list and lets them judge.

## Hooks

> Hooks communicate the angle. Keep them DIRECT (not indirect).
> Each hook should be usable as a headline on a static ad or opening line of a video.

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "Plant-based doesn't mean harmless": agree; that is why the full list is printed, not summarised
- "A pod is exactly the kind of thing a toddler puts in their mouth": one line on the lidded tin kept up high
