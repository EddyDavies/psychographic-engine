# Sub-Avatar: sub-toddler-opening-cupboards

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

## Parent Core Avatar
[core-careful-parent](../profile.md)

## Niche Gap
- **Situation:** Their toddler has just learned to open the cupboard under the sink, and they are childproofing the kitchen this week.
- **Unique Objection:** "A pod is exactly the kind of thing a toddler puts in their mouth."
- **Different Value Prop:** The value is fewer things to hide: four bottles they filled themselves from a short ingredient list, and one lidded tin that lives on a high shelf, instead of a cupboard of mixed bottles.
- **Three Questions:** different ad: yes (the cupboard under the sink, the high shelf, storage); unique objection: yes (pods look like sweets); situation alone: yes (toddler just started opening cupboards)

> The niche gap test (see `../../README.md`). All three must be yes. A "no" means the gap is weak: name the sub-avatar it overlaps and merge rather than keep it.

## Layered Categories
> Minimum 2 Core 5 categories combined
1. **Emotion:** Unease about what is in the bottles a child can reach
2. **Experience:** Toddler has just started opening cupboards
3. **Behaviour:** Emptying the cupboard under the sink onto the top of the fridge, fitting locks

## Full Description
> One sentence combining all categories:
"They want to know what's in every bottle their child can reach, but their toddler has just learned to open the cupboard under the sink, so they feel a jolt of worry every time it bangs open and have moved everything onto the top of the fridge."

## Implied Beliefs
> Decomposed from the belief "the fewer bottles in reach, the better":
- **Experience:** "Because the toddler opened the cupboard under the sink and pulled out a bottle..."
- **Emotion:** "...they feel a jolt of fear and then guilt for not having moved it sooner..."
- **Behavior:** "...so they clear the cupboard, stack bottles on the fridge, and avoid buying anything new that adds to the pile."

## Hooks That Resonate
1. "Four bottles. One tin. One high shelf.": the new, simpler cupboard in one line
2. "She opened the cupboard under the sink.": the exact moment, no product, no scare
3. "Know every word on the label.": the short ingredient list as relief
