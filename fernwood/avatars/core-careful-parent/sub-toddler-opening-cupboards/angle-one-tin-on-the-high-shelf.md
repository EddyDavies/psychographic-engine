# Angle: One Tin on the High Shelf

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** The Starter Kit swaps a cupboard of mixed bottles for four bottles and one lidded tin that lives on a high shelf, so childproofing the kitchen is one thing to put away instead of eleven.

**Emotional Lever:** The jolt when the cupboard under the sink bangs open, and the overwhelm of not knowing which bottles to worry about. Profile: Experience "toddler has just started opening cupboards", Behaviour "emptying the cupboard under the sink onto the top of the fridge", and the belief "the fewer bottles in reach, the better". The angle offers fewer things to manage, not a safety promise.

## Proof Points

> Only claims the product or offer file supports, each with where it comes from. Never fabricate volumes, names, or stories (see `engine/method/copywriting.md`).

- Aluminium pod tin with a push-and-twist lid (source: `offers/starter-kit.md`)
- Four 500ml bottles with silicone sleeves (source: `offers/starter-kit.md`)
- One pod replaces one ready-mixed bottle (source: `products/shared-mechanisms.md`)
- No chlorine bleach, no ammonia in any pod (source: `products/shared-mechanisms.md`)
- Not claimed: child-safe, non-toxic, safe if swallowed (see `brand/no-gos/no-gos.md`). Storage guidance stays "keep out of reach of children"

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "A pod is exactly the kind of thing a toddler puts in their mouth": agree, which is why they live in one lidded tin on a high shelf, not loose in a cupboard; never show a pod near a child
- "Glass bottles in a house with a toddler?": silicone sleeves, and the pods work in any plastic bottle they already own
