# Sub-Avatar: sub-let-down-eco-switcher

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

## Parent Core Avatar
[core-low-waste-household](../profile.md)

## Niche Gap
- **Situation:** She switched to an eco spray in the last year or two, the windows and mirrors smeared, and she went back to her old supermarket spray.
- **Unique Objection:** "Eco products never clean as well."
- **Different Value Prop:** The product has to prove the clean, not the planet; she already wants the planet part and has been burned by it.
- **Three Questions:** different ad: yes (a glass demo, not a plastic message); unique objection: yes ("eco products never clean as well"); situation alone: yes (switched once, switched back)

> The niche gap test (see `../../README.md`). All three must be yes. A "no" means the gap is weak: name the sub-avatar it overlaps and merge rather than keep it.

## Layered Categories
> Minimum 2 Core 5 categories combined
1. **Desire:** Stop buying plastic bottles without the house looking grubby
2. **Experience:** Tried an eco spray; it smeared the glass; went back to the old one
3. **Behaviour:** Tests every new cleaner on the bathroom mirror first

## Full Description
> One sentence combining all categories:
"She wants to stop buying plastic bottles, but the eco spray she tried smeared every mirror in the house, so she feels let down and a bit foolish and now tests anything green on the glass before she believes a word of it."

## Implied Beliefs
> Decomposed from the belief "eco products never clean as well":
- **Experience:** "Because her eco spray left the windows streaky and she had to go back..."
- **Emotion:** "...she feels let down, even conned for trying to do the right thing..."
- **Behavior:** "...so she ignores green claims, reads reviews for 'but does it clean', and judges any new spray on the mirror."

## Hooks That Resonate
1. "One pod. Tap water. Streak-free glass.": the mechanism and the proof in her own test, with no planet talk
2. "Finally an eco spray that actually cleans.": her own words back to her; validates the let-down
3. "Most of your spray bottle is water.": new information that explains why the last one failed without blaming her

## Awareness Level
**Solution Aware**

## Objections
> Specific to this sub-avatar
- "Eco products never clean as well": she has direct proof they don't
- "I've already wasted money on one of these": fear of being fooled twice
- "It'll be fine on the worktop but useless on glass": glass is where her last one failed
