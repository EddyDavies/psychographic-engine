# Angle: Your Last Eco Spray Was Mostly Water

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** Her last eco spray failed because it was a weak, ready-mixed liquid, and the Starter Kit's pods put a full dose in every bottle, so this switch is different for a reason she can understand.

**Emotional Lever:** The quiet self-blame behind "I felt like I'd been conned for being a good person." Profile: Emotion layer and the objection "I've already wasted money on one of these". New information gives her a reason the first switch failed that is not her fault and not "eco products are rubbish", which leaves room to try again without feeling foolish.

## Hooks

> Hooks communicate the angle. Keep them DIRECT (not indirect).
> Each hook should be usable as a headline on a static ad or opening line of a video.

1. **"Most of your spray bottle is water."**
   inspired_by: new information (`products/all-purpose-spray.md`, Assumed New Information); `engine/method/copywriting.md` hook sources

2. **"It wasn't you. It was the dose."**
   inspired_by: review mining, "conned for being a good person"; validates the pain

3. **"We stopped posting you water."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm from the mechanism (`products/shared-mechanisms.md`, you add the water)

## Proof Points

> Only claims the product or offer file supports, each with where it comes from. Never fabricate volumes, names, or stories (see `engine/method/copywriting.md`).

- Ready-mixed spray is mostly water (source: `products/all-purpose-spray.md`, Assumed New Information; general category fact, no percentage claimed)
- One pod makes one 500ml bottle at a fixed, full dose (source: `products/all-purpose-spray.md`)
- Streaks are leftover residue; a weak spray makes you spray more (source: `products/glass-spray.md`, Assumed New Information)
- 30-day clean-or-refund guarantee (source: `offers/starter-kit.md`)

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "I've already wasted money on one of these": the explanation (it was the dose) plus the guarantee
- "Eco products never clean as well": agree that weak ones don't, then show what a full dose does on glass
