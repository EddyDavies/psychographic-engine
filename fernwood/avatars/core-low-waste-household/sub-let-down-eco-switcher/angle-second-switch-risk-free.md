# Angle: Second Switch, No Risk

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** If the Starter Kit doesn't clean as well as the spray she went back to, she gets her money back and keeps the bottles, so trying green a second time costs her nothing.

**Emotional Lever:** Fear of being fooled twice. Profile: Objection "I've already wasted money on one of these" and the Implied Belief that green claims are not to be trusted. A guarantee measured against her own old spray puts the judgement in her hands, which is the only judge she trusts now.

## Hooks

> Hooks communicate the angle. Keep them DIRECT (not indirect).
> Each hook should be usable as a headline on a static ad or opening line of a video.

1. **"As clean as your old spray, or free."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm from the guarantee (`offers/starter-kit.md`)

2. **"You be the judge. Use the mirror."**
   inspired_by: review mining, "The mirror is my test"

3. **"Went back to the old stuff? Fair."**
   inspired_by: review mining, "Back to the old stuff"; validates the pain in her words

## Proof Points

> Only claims the product or offer file supports, each with where it comes from. Never fabricate volumes, names, or stories (see `engine/method/copywriting.md`).

- 30-day clean-or-refund guarantee: full refund of the kit, nothing to send back (source: `offers/starter-kit.md`)
- Keep the four glass bottles either way (source: `offers/starter-kit.md`, value proposition)
- Streak-free on glass with the included cloth (source: `products/glass-spray.md`)

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "I've already wasted money on one of these": this time the refund is the default if it fails
- "Eco products never clean as well": she does not have to believe us; she compares it to her old spray
