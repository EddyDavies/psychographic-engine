# Angle: Get the Cleaning Shelf Back

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** Four bottles and one pocket-sized tin of pods replace the shared shelf of half-used plastic sprays, so the flat gets its shelf back.

**Emotional Lever:** The daily irritation of the shared shelf. Profile: Experience "one shared cleaning shelf" and the golden phrase "We have one shelf for four people's cleaning stuff and it's a war zone." The win is visible and immediate, and it is a win for the whole flat, which they rarely get to buy.

## Proof Points

> Only claims the product or offer file supports, each with where it comes from. Never fabricate volumes, names, or stories (see `engine/method/copywriting.md`).

- Four 500ml glass bottles, one tin, 12 pods in the kit (source: `offers/starter-kit.md`)
- The pod tin fits in a coat pocket; a year of spray takes less room than one ready-mixed bottle (source: `products/all-purpose-spray.md`, Hidden Mechanisms)
- One pod replaces one plastic bottle (source: `products/shared-mechanisms.md`)
- Founder story: two flatmates counted 23 bottles under a shared sink (source: `products/shared-mechanisms.md`)

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "My flatmates will use it and never refill it": one line in the body, "drop a pod in, add water, that's it", carried in full by `angle-refill-in-ten-seconds`
