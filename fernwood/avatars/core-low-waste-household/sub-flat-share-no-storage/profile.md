# Sub-Avatar: sub-flat-share-no-storage

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

## Parent Core Avatar
[core-low-waste-household](../profile.md)

## Niche Gap
- **Situation:** Shares a rented flat with two to four others and one shelf of cleaning supplies, which is full of half-used bottles that nobody owns and nobody replaces.
- **Unique Objection:** "My flatmates will use it and never refill it."
- **Different Value Prop:** The value is space and fairness in a shared home: one tin and four bottles anyone can refill in ten seconds, and it all moves with you when the tenancy ends.
- **Three Questions:** different ad: yes (shared shelf, flatmates, moving out); unique objection: yes (flatmates won't refill it); situation alone: yes (shared flat, one shelf)

> The niche gap test (see `../../README.md`). All three must be yes. A "no" means the gap is weak: name the sub-avatar it overlaps and merge rather than keep it.

## Layered Categories
> Minimum 2 Core 5 categories combined
1. **Desire:** Get the shelf back and stop buying plastic bottles
2. **Experience:** Lives in a flat share with one shared cleaning shelf
3. **Emotion:** Low-level resentment at being the only one who buys supplies

## Full Description
> One sentence combining all categories:
"They want a tidy shelf and less plastic, but they share one cupboard with flatmates who never replace anything, so they feel quietly resentful and keep buying the spray themselves while the duplicates pile up."

## Implied Beliefs
> Decomposed from the belief "anything shared in this flat gets wrecked":
- **Experience:** "Because they've bought spray that got used up and never replaced..."
- **Emotion:** "...they feel resentful and a bit taken for granted..."
- **Behavior:** "...so they keep their own stuff in their room, and won't spend on anything nice for the shared kitchen."

## Hooks That Resonate
1. "One shelf. Four flatmates. Eleven bottles.": names the scene exactly
2. "Even your flatmates can refill this.": answers their unique objection with a wink
3. "A year of spray in one tin.": the space mechanism in one line

## Awareness Level
**Problem Aware**

## Objections
> Specific to this sub-avatar
- "My flatmates will use it and never refill it": refilling has always fallen on them
- "I'm not buying nice glass bottles for a flat I'm leaving next year": short tenancy, no investment in the space
- "There's literally no room for another thing": space is the problem, not the solution
