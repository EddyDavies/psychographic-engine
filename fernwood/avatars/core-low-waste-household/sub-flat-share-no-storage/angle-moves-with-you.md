# Angle: The Kit Moves With You

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** The four bottles and the tin are theirs to keep and pack in one box when the tenancy ends, so it is the last cleaning stuff they buy for a flat, not money left behind for the next tenants.

**Emotional Lever:** Reluctance to spend on a space that is not theirs. Profile: Objection "I'm not buying nice glass bottles for a flat I'm leaving next year." The angle reframes the kit from a purchase for the flat into a purchase for them.

## Hooks

> Hooks communicate the angle. Keep them DIRECT (not indirect).
> Each hook should be usable as a headline on a static ad or opening line of a video.

1. **"The last spray bottles you'll leave behind."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm from the profile objection (short tenancy)

2. **"Four bottles. One box. Every move."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm; review mining, moving flat as an urgency event

3. **"Moving out? Don't buy cleaning stuff again."**
   inspired_by: review mining, emotional triggers (moving flat)

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "I'm not buying nice glass bottles for a flat I'm leaving next year": they are not for the flat; they go in the box with you
- "There's literally no room for another thing": the kit replaces the shelf of plastic, it does not add to it
