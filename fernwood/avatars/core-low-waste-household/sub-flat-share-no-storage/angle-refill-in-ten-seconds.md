# Angle: Even Your Flatmates Can Refill It

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** Refilling is drop a pod in and add tap water, so whoever empties the bottle can refill it on the spot and the job stops falling on them.

**Emotional Lever:** Quiet resentment at being the only one who replaces supplies. Profile: Emotion layer and the unique objection "My flatmates will use it and never refill it." The angle turns the flatmates' laziness into the proof: it is so easy that even they will do it.

## Hooks

> Hooks communicate the angle. Keep them DIRECT (not indirect).
> Each hook should be usable as a headline on a static ad or opening line of a video.

1. **"Even your flatmates can refill this."**
   inspired_by: review mining, "Even my flatmates refill them"

2. **"Whoever finishes it, refills it."**
   inspired_by: review mining, "Whoever finishes the spray never buys the next one"

3. **"Pod in. Tap on. Done."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm from the mechanism (`products/all-purpose-spray.md`, 6th grade version)

## Proof Points

> Only claims the product or offer file supports, each with where it comes from. Never fabricate volumes, names, or stories (see `engine/method/copywriting.md`).

- The tin holds the pods on the shelf next to the bottles; no shop trip (source: `offers/starter-kit.md`)

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "My flatmates will use it and never refill it": the refill is quicker than complaining about it; show a flatmate doing it half asleep
- "There's literally no room for another thing": one tin, four bottles, nothing else
