# Angle: Pods Don't Go Off

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** Dry pods keep for two years in the tin, so buying the kit can never turn into a drawer of refills going to waste.

**Emotional Lever:** The memory of the pile-up. Profile: Objection "I'll end up with a drawer of refills I don't need" and Experience "a past subscription piled up". For this sub-avatar the pain was never price; it was the waste and the feeling of being foolish, and a product that cannot go off removes both.

## Hooks

> Hooks communicate the angle. Keep them DIRECT (not indirect).
> Each hook should be usable as a headline on a static ad or opening line of a video.

1. **"Pods don't go off."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm from the product (`products/all-purpose-spray.md`, dry pod keeps for two years)

2. **"No drawer of refills. Ever."**
   inspired_by: review mining, "a drawer full of razor heads I never asked for"

3. **"Use one when you need one."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm; control over pace instead of a schedule

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "I'll end up with a drawer of refills I don't need": nothing arrives unless you order it, and what you have keeps
- "It's just going to be another subscription I can't get out of": one line, "one-off kit, nothing renews", carried in full by `angle-buy-once-nothing-renews`
