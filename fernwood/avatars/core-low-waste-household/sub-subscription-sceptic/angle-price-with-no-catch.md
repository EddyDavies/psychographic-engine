# Angle: The Price, With No Catch

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** The Starter Kit is £24 and every price after it is printed up front (93p a bottle one-off), so there is no hidden plan behind the low entry price.

**Emotional Lever:** Suspicion of cheap entry prices. Profile: Objection "What's the catch with the cheap kit price?" and the Behaviour of looking for the catch. Showing the whole price picture, including the boring part, is the reassurance.

## Hooks

> Hooks communicate the angle. Keep them DIRECT (not indirect).
> Each hook should be usable as a headline on a static ad or opening line of a video.

1. **"£24. That's the whole catch."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm from the profile objection

2. **"Every price, up front."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm; review mining, "If I can't cancel it in two clicks I'm not signing up"

3. **"No trial. No plan. Just £24."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm; negates the trial-to-subscription pattern she fears

> Only claims the product or offer file supports, each with where it comes from. Never fabricate volumes, names, or stories (see `engine/method/copywriting.md`).

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "What's the catch with the cheap kit price?": show the after-price in the ad, not just the kit price
- "It's just going to be another subscription I can't get out of": "nothing renews" next to the price
