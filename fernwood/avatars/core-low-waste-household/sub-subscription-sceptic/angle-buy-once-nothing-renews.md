# Angle: Buy It Once, Nothing Renews

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

**Reason to Buy:** The Starter Kit is a one-off purchase with no plan attached, so they can switch to refills without signing up to anything.

**Emotional Lever:** Wariness from being burned before. Profile: Implied Belief "refill brands just want to lock you in" and Behaviour "checks cancellation terms and 'does this renew?' before buying anything". The angle answers the question they check first, in the first line, which is the opposite of what `refill-not-reorder` did.

## Hooks

> Hooks communicate the angle. Keep them DIRECT (not indirect).
> Each hook should be usable as a headline on a static ad or opening line of a video.

1. **"Buy it once. Nothing renews."**
   inspired_by: learning from `refill-not-reorder` (c01, c02); review mining, "I checked twice that nothing renews"

2. **"Refills without the subscription."**
   inspired_by: brainstorm from the offer type (`offers/starter-kit.md`)

3. **"Is this a subscription? No."**
   inspired_by: c01 ad comments, "Is this a subscription? Not falling for that again"

## Proof Points

> Only claims the product or offer file supports, each with where it comes from. Never fabricate volumes, names, or stories (see `engine/method/copywriting.md`).

- The kit is a one-off purchase; nothing renews (source: `offers/starter-kit.md`, Objection Pre-emption)
- Pods can be bought later as single tins of 15 for £14 (source: `offers/refill-subscription.md`, regular price)
- Pods work in any 500ml spray bottle (source: `products/all-purpose-spray.md`)

## Objections to Pre-empt

> Taken from the sub-avatar's Objections section. Body copy answers each with Objection, Claim, Proof, Benefit.

- "It's just going to be another subscription I can't get out of": answered in the hook itself
- "What's the catch with the cheap kit price?": state plainly what pods cost afterwards (93p a bottle one-off), so there is no catch left to find
