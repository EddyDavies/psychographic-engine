# Core Avatar: core-low-waste-household

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand; quotes come from invented research.

## Anchor Category
**Desire:** "I want to stop putting a plastic spray bottle in the recycling every time one runs out, without my house looking grubby."

## Mass Instinct
Status, in the self-respect sense: being the household that quietly does its bit and is not fooled by green marketing. Comfort (a proper clean, no hassle) sits underneath as the condition that must not be traded away.

## Core 5 Profile

### Desires
- **Primary:** Stop buying a new plastic bottle every time the spray runs out
- Go green without the cleaning getting worse
- A cleaning cupboard that is four things, not eleven
- Feel like the thoughtful, organised one in the house without making a thing of it

### Experiences
- Has a cupboard or shelf of half-used plastic sprays
- Has tried at least one eco spray or refill, often with a worse result on glass
- Puts plastic in the recycling and has read that much of it is not recycled
- Has bought refill or eco products that turned out to be mostly marketing
- Shops online for household basics and is used to letterbox deliveries

### Emotions
**Primary Emotions:**
- Sadness (guilt): triggered by the recycling bin filling with spray bottles
- Anger (let-down): triggered by an eco product that did not work after paying more for it

**Secondary Emotions:**
- Sceptical: because "eco" on a label has meant very little before (Fear)
- Quietly proud: because small, practical changes feel like doing their bit (Joy)
- Embarrassed: because a smeared mirror or a grubby kitchen gets noticed when people come round (Fear)

### Behaviors
- Tests any new cleaner on glass first ("The mirror is my test")
- Reads reviews looking for "but does it actually clean"
- Rinses and recycles bottles, keeps refill pouches, buys loose fruit
- Goes back to the old spray quietly when the green one fails, and does not tell anyone

### Demographics
> Broad strokes only. Often implied by the above.
- 28 to 45
- Any gender; the buyer is usually whoever does most of the cleaning
- UK, renting or in a first home, often in a city

## Awareness Level
**Solution Aware**

> - **Unaware:** Doesn't know they have a problem
> - **Problem Aware:** Knows the problem, not the solutions
> - **Solution Aware:** Knows solutions exist, doesn't know your product
> - **Product Aware:** Knows your product, hasn't bought yet
> - **Most Aware:** Knows your product well, needs a push to buy
