# Fernwood Starter Kit

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand. Offer slug: `starter-kit`. Reference photo: `images/fernwood-starter-kit.png` (not included in this example).

## Basics
- **Products Included:**
  - 4 x 500ml glass spray bottles with sage silicone sleeves (one per room)
  - 1 aluminium pod tin with a push-and-twist lid
  - 12 pods: 6 [all-purpose](../products/all-purpose-spray.md) (choose Lemon Peel or Unscented), 3 [glass & mirror](../products/glass-spray.md), 3 [bathroom](../products/bathroom-spray.md)
  - 1 microfibre glass cloth
  - Kraft paper box
- **Regular Price:** £31 (4 bottles at £4.50, tin and cloth £2, 12 pods at 93p, rounded)
- **Offer Price:** £24
- **Discount:** About 23% off buying the parts separately
- **Availability:** Always available; first-time customers only get the 30-day clean-or-refund guarantee

## Offer Type
Bundle (starter kit) with a risk-reversal guarantee

## Value Proposition
> Four glass bottles, a tin and your first 12 bottles of spray for £24, and if it doesn't clean as well as what you used before, we refund the kit and you keep the bottles.

## Guarantee
- **Clean or refund, 30 days:** if the spray doesn't clean as well as what you used before, email us within 30 days for a full refund of the kit. No need to send anything back

## Objection Pre-emption
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I only need one product" | Pods work in any 500ml bottle you already own, so a tin on its own is an option; the kit is for people who want the glass bottles and the first round of every pod |
| "It's too expensive" | £24 is 12 bottles of spray plus four glass bottles you keep. After that a bottle costs 80p to 93p |
| "Eco products never clean as well" | The 30-day clean-or-refund guarantee, and the glass cloth so you can see it on the mirror |
| "Glass bottles in my house?" | Silicone sleeves on every bottle; the pods work in any plastic bottle you already have if you prefer |
| "I'll get locked into a subscription" | The kit is a one-off purchase. Nothing renews |
