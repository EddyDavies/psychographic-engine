# Fernwood Refill Subscription

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand. Offer slug: `refill-subscription`.

## Basics
- **Products Included:** 15 pods every 3 months, any mix of [all-purpose](../products/all-purpose-spray.md), [glass & mirror](../products/glass-spray.md) and [bathroom](../products/bathroom-spray.md), in a letterbox-sized paper pouch
- **Regular Price:** £14 a tin of 15 pods one-off
- **Offer Price:** £12 a delivery (80p a bottle), free UK delivery
- **Discount:** About 14% off one-off tins, plus free delivery
- **Availability:** Always available; existing customers or alongside a Starter Kit

## Offer Type
Subscription (skip, pause, change the mix or cancel online any time)

## Value Proposition
> 60 pods a year for the price of 12 bottles of a typical £4 supermarket spray, through the letterbox, and you can skip a delivery from your account the moment your tin is still full.

## Target Avatars
| Avatar | Why This Offer Appeals |
|---|---|
| `sub-weaning-high-chair` | Wipes the tray and floor after every meal and goes through all-purpose pods fastest; running out mid-mess is the real pain |
| `sub-let-down-eco-switcher` | Once the glass test has convinced her, the subscription is the cheapest way to keep going (Most Aware customers only) |
| `sub-subscription-sceptic` | Do not target with this offer. See `testing/learnings.md` (`refill-not-reorder`) |

## Objection Pre-emption
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I'll end up with a drawer of pods" | Skip or push back a delivery from your account; dry pods keep for two years, so nothing goes off in the meantime |
| "It's too expensive" | £12 a quarter, 80p a bottle; £48 a year is what 12 bottles of a £4 supermarket spray cost |
| "I can just buy a tin when I run out" | You can. The subscription is cheaper per pod and you never find the bottle empty mid-wipe |
| "Cancelling will be a nightmare" | Cancel online in your account, no phone call, no email chain |
