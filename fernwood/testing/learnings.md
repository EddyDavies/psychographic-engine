## Key Learnings

1. `sub-let-down-eco-switcher` responds to proof that the clean is as good as before, not to the planet. The hook hit her experience of eco spray smearing the glass (c01). Glass is her own test, confirmed by c01 comments (`../avatars/research/ad-comments-c01-2026-08-20.md`).
2. For `sub-subscription-sceptic`, convenience is the threat, not the benefit. Any hook that implies something arrives on its own raises their objection instead of answering it (c01, c02). Lead with ownership and no commitment.
3. A static that shows the product but not the situation loses for situation-based sub-avatars even when the angle is right (c01, `cupboard-space-back`). The shared shelf has to be in the frame.
