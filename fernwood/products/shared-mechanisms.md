# Shared Mechanisms

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

Brand-level mechanisms that apply across all products:

### Manufacturing / Process
- Every pod is a dry, compressed concentrate pressed in the UK. You add the water at home, so Fernwood never ships water or bottles after the first kit
- Fixed dose per pod: one pod, one 500ml bottle, the same strength every time

### Founder Story
- Started in 2021 by two flatmates in Leeds who cleared out the cupboard under their shared sink and counted 23 plastic spray bottles, most of them half used. They had both tried eco sprays and gone back because the windows smeared. Fernwood was the attempt to make a refill that cleaned like the bottle they gave up

### Business Model
- Direct to consumer. One-off Starter Kit (bottles, tin, first pods), then pods by letterbox: one-off tins or a refill subscription that can be skipped, paused or cancelled online
- Pods ship flat in a paper pouch that fits through the letterbox

### Sourcing / Ingredients
- Plant-derived cleaning agents, citric acid and sodium bicarbonate; lemon peel oil in the Lemon Peel all-purpose pod, eucalyptus oil in the bathroom pod
- No chlorine bleach, no ammonia
- Full ingredient list printed on the tin and the product page

### Certifications / Social Proof
- Over 4,000 five-star reviews on the Fernwood site
- 30-day clean-or-refund guarantee on the Starter Kit
- One pod replaces one plastic bottle (the specific environmental claim we are allowed to make)
- No third-party certifications claimed; do not imply any
