## How It Works
A pod made for glass, mirrors and shower screens. Dissolve one in 500ml of tap water, spray, and wipe once with the dry microfibre cloth that comes in the Starter Kit. The formula dries fast and evenly, so it does not leave the cloudy film that makes glass look smeared.

**6th grade version:** Drop one pod in the bottle and add tap water, which makes a glass spray that wipes off clear in one go.

## Features
- One pod makes one 500ml bottle
- Plant-based formula with a fast-drying, low-residue blend
- Streak-free on glass and mirrors when wiped with a dry microfibre cloth
- No ammonia, no added fragrance
- Starter Kit includes one microfibre glass cloth

## Assumed Unique Mechanisms
> What makes this product's approach different from competitors?
- Low-residue blend at full dose: streaks on glass usually come from leftover cleaning agent drying unevenly, and a fixed dose stops people over-spraying a weak mix to compensate

## Assumed New Information
> Novel claims the market hasn't heard. What can we teach them?
- Streaks are leftover residue, not dirt. A weak spray makes you spray more, which leaves more residue
- Glass is the fastest way to see if any cleaner works: it hides nothing
