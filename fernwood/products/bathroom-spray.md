- **SKU:** FW-POD-BA

## How It Works
A citric-acid based pod for sinks, taps, tiles, baths and shower screens. Dissolve one in 500ml of tap water, spray, leave for a minute on limescale, then wipe. It lifts everyday soap scum and light limescale without chlorine bleach.

**6th grade version:** Drop one pod in the bottle and add tap water, which makes a bathroom spray that shifts limescale without the bleach smell.

## Features
- One pod makes one 500ml bottle
- Plant-based formula with citric acid for limescale
- No chlorine bleach, no ammonia
- Light eucalyptus scent from eucalyptus oil
- For sinks, taps, tiles, baths and shower screens; not for natural stone or marble

## Assumed New Information
> Novel claims the market hasn't heard. What can we teach them?

- The "clean smell" of a bathroom spray is mostly the smell of the product, not of clean
