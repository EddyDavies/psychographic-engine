# All-Purpose Spray Pod

> Fictional worked example. Fernwood is not a real brand.

## Basics
- **SKU:** FW-POD-AP-LEM (Lemon Peel), FW-POD-AP-UNS (Unscented)
- **Category:** Household cleaning, refill concentrate
- **Price:** 80p a pod on the refill subscription (15 pods for £12); 93p a pod one-off (15 pods for £14). Also in the Starter Kit
- **URL:** fernwood.example/all-purpose (fictional)

## How It Works
A compressed tablet of plant-based cleaning concentrate. Drop one pod into any 500ml spray bottle, fill with tap water, and wait for the fizzing to stop. You now have a full bottle of all-purpose spray for worktops, tables, hobs, sinks, high chair trays and painted surfaces.

**6th grade version:** Drop one pod in the bottle and add tap water, which makes a full bottle of spray without buying a new one.

## Features
- One pod makes one 500ml bottle of spray
- Plant-based formula: plant-derived cleaning agents, citric acid, sodium bicarbonate
- Two versions: Lemon Peel (scented with lemon peel oil) and Unscented (no added fragrance)
- No chlorine bleach, no ammonia
- Cuts everyday kitchen grease (food smears, hob splashes) in one or two wipes
- Full ingredient list printed on the tin and on the product page
- Dry pod keeps for two years sealed
- Works in any 500ml spray bottle, glass or plastic
- Made in the UK

## Assumed Benefits
> Marked "assumed" until validated by customer feedback or testing.
- No new plastic bottle every time you run out (one pod replaces one bottle)
- Takes up a fraction of the cupboard space of ready-mixed sprays
- No harsh chemical smell hanging around after you wipe the kitchen
- You know exactly what is in the bottle, because you made it and the list is short
- Spray is cheap enough per bottle that you never ration it
- Unscented version leaves nothing perfumed on surfaces where food goes

## Assumed Desires
> Map to Mass Instincts: Health | Sex | Status | Belonging | Control | Comfort

- **Comfort:** a proper clean without the smell, the trip to the shop or the clutter
- **Status:** be the thoughtful, organised one in the house who quietly stopped buying plastic
- **Health:** feel settled about what is on the surfaces the family eats from (never claimed as a safety benefit; see no-gos)
- **Control:** never run out, never wonder what is in the bottle

## Assumed Unique Mechanisms
> What makes this product's approach different from competitors?
- Full-dose concentrate in a dry pod: every bottle gets the same strength of cleaner, because you are not buying a diluted liquid
- You supply the water and the bottle; Fernwood only posts the part that cleans

## Assumed New Information
> Novel claims the market hasn't heard. What can we teach them?
- Ready-mixed spray is mostly water. You are paying to ship water and a new bottle every time
- A weak eco spray is usually a dosing problem, not a plant-based problem

## Hidden Mechanisms
> Things the customer doesn't know but would find compelling if revealed.
- Because the dose is fixed per pod, the spray is the same strength in month six as it was on day one; nothing settles or separates
- The pod tin fits in a coat pocket: a year of spray takes less room than one ready-mixed bottle
- If it worked too well: customers say they "use it on everything" and run out of all-purpose pods first
- Explained to a friend: "It's a fizzy tablet. You chuck it in the bottle you've already got."
